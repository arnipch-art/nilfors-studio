Tumble Nilfors Studio

Rotate the world.

You never move a piece. You turn the whole board, and everything falls somewhere new.

In development

One swipe. Everything falls. Three of a colour clear.

One gesture

Swipe left or right to turn the board a quarter turn. Tap to pass. That's the whole control scheme.

See it before you commit

Hold a rotation and the board shows a ghost of exactly where every block will land, cascades included. Let go to play it, or back out.

Gravity does the rest

Blocks fall, groups of three or more clear, and the gaps let the pile fall again. Long chains are worth far more than single clears.

No timer, no reflexes. Every move waits for you, so it plays the same whether you have four seconds or four minutes.

Being built for iPhone and Android. Your progress stays on the device — there is nothing to sign into and no account to make.
