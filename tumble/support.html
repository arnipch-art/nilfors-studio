Common questions

How do I play?

Swipe left or right to turn the whole board a quarter turn. Tap to pass. That is the entire control scheme — you never pick up or move a single piece.

Can I see what a move will do before I commit to it?

Yes, and it is rather the point. Hold a rotation and the board shows a ghost of exactly where every block will land, cascades included. Let go to play it, or back out and try the other direction.

How do blocks clear?

Gravity pulls everything down after each turn. Three or more of the same colour touching will clear, and the gaps let the pile fall again. Long chains are worth far more than single clears.

Is there a time limit?

No. There is no timer and nothing depends on reaction speed. Every move waits for you, so it plays the same whether you have four seconds or four minutes.

Does Tumble need an internet connection?

The game itself does not. There is nothing to sign into, and your progress is kept on the device. The ads need a connection, so that is the one part of Tumble that goes online.

I got a new phone — does my progress come with me?

Not at the moment. Tumble keeps everything on the device and has no accounts or cloud saves, so progress does not follow you to a new phone.

Does Tumble collect my data?

We collect nothing — there is no account and no server of ours. The one thing that leaves your device is whatever the ad network needs to serve and measure ads. Full detail: Privacy Policy

When is it out?

Tumble is still in development for iPhone and Android. There is no release date yet.
