Privacy Policy

Isle · Nilfors Studio · Last updated 9 August 2026

The short version

Isle does not collect, store, or transmit anything about you. Your designs, your colours and your gem balance live on your iPhone and nowhere else. There is no server, no account system, and no way for us to identify you.

The one exception is advertising, described below.

What stays on your device

  • The designs you create, including drawings and photos you import
  • Your gem balance and which rewards you have claimed
  • Your chosen design, colour and label

All of this is stored locally on your iPhone, and in a shared container used by the Isle widget so it can draw on the Dynamic Island. None of it is uploaded. Deleting the app deletes all of it permanently, and we cannot recover it, because we never had it.

Photos

When you choose a photo, Isle reads only the image you select. It is processed entirely on your device, and what gets saved is a small copy you have edited. Isle has no access to the rest of your photo library.

Advertising

Isle shows rewarded video ads through Google AdMob, and only when you choose to watch one in exchange for gems. Google is an independent company, and its software may collect device and usage information in order to deliver and measure those ads.

Isle does not request App Tracking Transparency permission, so ads shown in Isle are non-personalised.

Google's handling of that data is governed by their policy, not ours: policies.google.com/privacy

Purchases

Gem purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Isle never sees your payment details, card number or billing address. We receive only Apple's confirmation that a purchase completed.

Children

Isle is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Since we collect no personal information from anyone, there is nothing for us to delete on request.

Your rights

Because Isle holds no personal data on any server, there is nothing to request, export, correct or erase from us. Everything Isle knows about you is on your device and under your control.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it, and material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
