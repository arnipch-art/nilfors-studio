# Privacy Policy

**Isle** · Nilfors Studio
Last updated: 9 August 2026

Host this at `https://nilfors.studio/isle/privacy.html` and paste that URL into App Store
Connect. A privacy policy URL is mandatory for any app that shows ads.

---

## The short version

Isle does not collect, store, or transmit anything about you. Your designs, your
colours and your gem balance live on your iPhone and nowhere else. We have no
server, no account system, and no way to identify you.

The one exception is advertising, described below.

---

## What stays on your device

- The designs you create, including drawings and photos you import
- Your gem balance and which rewards you have claimed
- Your chosen design, colour and label

All of this is stored locally on your iPhone and in a shared container used by
the Isle widget. None of it is uploaded. Deleting the app deletes all of it
permanently, and we cannot recover it because we never had it.

**Photos.** When you choose a photo, Isle reads only the image you select. It is
processed on your device, and the version saved is a small copy you have edited.
Isle has no access to the rest of your photo library.

---

## Advertising

Isle shows rewarded video ads through **Google AdMob**, and only when you choose
to watch one for gems. Google is an independent company and its SDK may collect
device and usage information to deliver and measure those ads.

Isle does **not** request App Tracking Transparency permission, so ads shown in
Isle are **non-personalised**.

Google's practices are governed by their own policy, not ours:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

---

## Purchases

Gem purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Isle never
sees your payment details, card number, or billing address. We receive only
Apple's confirmation that a purchase completed.

---

## Children

Isle is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect any
information from them. Since we collect no personal information from anyone,
there is nothing for us to delete on request.

---

## Your rights

Because Isle holds no personal data on any server, there is nothing to request,
export, correct or erase from us. Everything Isle knows about you is on your
device and under your control.

---

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and material
changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
