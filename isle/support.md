## Common questions

### My design disappeared from the Dynamic Island

iOS ends every Live Activity after about eight hours. Open Isle and tap **Put it
on the island** to bring it back. This applies to every app that uses the
Dynamic Island, and there is no way around it.

### I don't see the Dynamic Island

The Dynamic Island is hardware, and only iPhone 14 Pro and later have it. On
earlier iPhones your design appears on the Lock Screen instead.

### Nothing appears when I tap "Put it on the island"

Check **Settings › Isle › Live Activities** is switched on. Isle will tell you if
it's off, but it's easy to miss.

### My drawing looks like a blob up there

The island draws your design at roughly 20 points across. Thin lines vanish at
that size. The preview at the top of the drawing screen is exactly actual size,
so if it reads there, it will read on the island.

### Can I get my gems back?

Gems spent on a design are not refundable, but the design is yours permanently —
it stays in your Library and you can use it as often as you like. For purchase
refunds, Apple handles those: reportaproblem.apple.com

### I bought gems and they haven't arrived

Force-quit Isle and reopen it. Purchases finish restoring on launch. If they're
still missing, email me with the date and amount and I'll sort it.

### Does Isle collect my data?

No. Your designs and gem balance never leave your phone. Full detail:
[Privacy Policy](https://nilfors.studio/isle/privacy.html)
