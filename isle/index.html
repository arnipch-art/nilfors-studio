Or use a photo

Bring in any picture and rub out what you don't want. What's left becomes the shape — no box around it.

One thing worth knowing. iOS ends every Live Activity after about eight hours. When that happens, open Isle and tap once to put your design back. Every app that uses the Dynamic Island works this way, so we'd rather say it up front than have you find out.

The Dynamic Island is on iPhone 14 Pro and later. On earlier iPhones your design appears on the Lock Screen instead.
